# RESEARCH — Overgrowth: The Solo Founder Skill Tree

## 1. Visual reference: GrassChopper (not Vampire Survivors)
GrassChopper (Grass Choppers studio; Steam demo released Jun 4 2026, ~97% positive) is a 2D side-scrolling pixel-art action-incremental game: swing a scythe, collect gold from every blade, buy upgrades, push deeper into hostile grass biomes.
- **Loop:** "Cut. Earn. Upgrade. Repeat." → our loop: *Do the chapter → earn Gold → unlock deeper chapters.*
- **The upgrade tree is drawn as twisting plant vines**, not a node graph. Players and reviewers call it the game's best design touch. → Our tree is rendered as curving SVG vines with leaf joints growing out of a grass field. There are no straight connector lines.
- **Oxygen drains the deeper you push; go too far and you retreat.** → **Oxygen = runway (months)**. You type in your runway. The meter turns red under 6 months, and the tree suggests you "surface" (go back to consulting, pause the VC branch).
- **A grass root sits at the heart of each area, and that's the boss.** → Each arc ends in a **ROOT boss**: a real milestone (10 conversations, landing page live, first paying customer, PMF signal, ramen profitable or term sheet).
- **Biomes get denser the deeper you go.** → The arcs read left to right as biomes: Meadow → Thicket → Clearing → Deep Grass → Overgrowth.
- **Special attacks use charges that refill slowly.** → **Swing charges**: 3 "focus swings" per week. Starting a "Do this week" action spends one, and they refill each Monday. That caps you at 3 in-flight bets, which matches solo-founder bandwidth.
- **Upgrades cost a fortune.** → Unlocks cost Gold and completion pays more back, so the next ring is always one completed chapter away.

## 2. Gamification: what transfers, what backfires
- Duolingo's white paper credits streaks, frequent small rewards and visible progress for retention. Studies of Duolingo also document **"gamification misuse"** (Hadi Mogavi et al., 2022): users chase points instead of learning, and time spent doesn't predict proficiency.
- **Design rule:** you only earn Gold by checking a concrete **"Done when"** outcome (a real artifact or metric). Reading a chapter earns nothing. The board has no streaks and no leaderboards.
- Prerequisites mirror real dependencies: you can't run Product Hunt before the landing page boss falls.
- **Mutually exclusive forks** (runway source; Bootstrap vs Venture) make you pick a strategy. A free **Replant** (respec) keeps the choice from feeling punishing.

## 3. Curriculum sources that shaped the arcs
- **YC Startup School:** ideas → co-founders → talking to users / MVP → launch (again and again) → KPIs, pricing, growth → fundraising. Its 2026 12-week certificate follows the same order: validation, MVP, growth/retention, fundraising.
- **The Mom Test** (Rob Fitzpatrick): ask about the past and about money already spent, push for commitment, and ignore compliments.
- **Sean Ellis PMF test:** ≥40% "very disappointed" (used as the Deep Grass boss).
- **Launch playbooks** (Indie Hackers / Product Hunt guides, 2026): spend 4–6 weeks building an audience before launch. Line up 15–30 real supporters. Launch PH Tue–Thu at 12:01 AM PT. Treat Indie Hackers as a founder story, not a press release (comment there for 2–4 weeks first). Treat PH/HN as a one-day spike, never the strategy. Warm followers convert at 10–30%, strangers at 0.5–2%.
- **Default alive** (Paul Graham / Trevor Blackwell): the bootstrap boss math.

## 4. Channel guidance (Deep Grass vines)
For each platform: who it suits, the format, cadence, the first 30 days, and red flags. Paid ads are locked until you have a converting funnel and payment. Meta needs about 50 conversions a week to leave its learning phase, so a solo budget should start with Google Search exact-match on high-intent terms.

## 5. Modifiers & readiness = real milestones
- **Success modifiers** are passive buffs from finished chapters, worded as real advantages. Example: *Warm Audience: launch conversion ×3*, granted by Build in Public. They show in a buff bar like the chopper's upgrade stats.
- **Launch Readiness** is a weighted checklist made of the gate nodes (positioning, landing page, payments, analytics, onboarding, legal basics, support, supporters list). The bar fills only from completed outcomes.
